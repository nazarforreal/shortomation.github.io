Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Shortomation ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website nazarzhmurko.com (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: We use Google Analytics to collect information about how you interact with our Site (e.g., pages visited, time spent, device type). This data is anonymized and helps us improve our user experience.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to store your preferences (such as cookie consent) and for analytics purposes. See our Cookie Policy for details.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us via email or LinkedIn, we will receive your email address and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Newsletter Data: We use Substack for our newsletter. When you subscribe, your email address is processed by Substack in accordance with their privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain our Site.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve our content.
  • To communicate with you (if you reach out to us).
  • To send newsletters (if you subscribe).

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect data independently:

  • Google Analytics: For website traffic analysis.
  • Substack: For newsletter management.
  • Gumroad & Payhip: For selling digital products. When you make a purchase, your transaction is processed by these platforms, and we do not store your payment information.

4. Data Retention

We retain non-personal analytics data for a limited period as defined by Google Analytics retention settings. Personal data provided via contact is kept only as long as necessary to address your inquiry.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., EU, California), you may have rights regarding your data, including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data.
  • The right to withdraw consent for cookies at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our LinkedIn profile.
